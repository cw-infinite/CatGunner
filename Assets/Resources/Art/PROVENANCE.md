# Original art pass v2 — 2026-10-02

The October 3 skin, gun and scenery expansion is documented in [EXPANSION_PROMPTS.md](EXPANSION_PROMPTS.md), including all generation/edit prompts and saved asset filenames.

These two transparent PNG atlases were generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. Reference video frames were supplied as style guidance only. No sprites were extracted from the reference recording and no third-party asset pack was imported.

- `world-v2.png`: six vegetation variants, original cream-and-ginger cat, separate blaster.
- `ui-v2.png`: button, menu, equipment card, currency pill, banknote, crystal, power and speed icons.

Runtime atlas regions and alpha trimming are defined in `SpriteArt.cs`. The loader preserves source alpha and shares atlas textures across sprites. Panel sprites use nine-slice borders. `ArtImport.cs` sets readable, uncompressed, non-mipmapped imports for deterministic region trimming. Review mobile memory and texture compression during Android profiling.

## World atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D Unity sprite atlas, transparent background. Create ONE landscape atlas with exactly 4 equal columns and 2 equal rows, eight separate sprites, one centered in each cell with generous transparent gutters. Reference image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY from a mobile gameplay recording: match rounded hand-drawn cartoon readability, warm dark-brown thick smooth outlines, flat pastel fills with one subtle shade, slight 3/4 top-down game perspective. Create original drawings, do not trace. Row 1 left to right: a squat rounded scalloped evergreen fir with 3 drooping rounded tiers and short brown trunk; round leafy green deciduous tree with scalloped broccoli-like crown and short pale trunk; pink flowering tree with rounded clustered blossom crown and short trunk; tropical palm with broad softly curved green fronds and short segmented tan trunk. Row 2 left to right: friendly rounded three-arm cactus with sparse short spines; large golden-leaf deciduous tree with ochre trunk and scalloped gold crown; original cute small cream-and-ginger CAT adventurer, triangular cat ears NOT rabbit, tiny paws, short curved tail, small olive neckerchief, full body facing right in three-quarter view, no weapon (separate gun drawn in final cell); simple short cartoon charcoal-and-walnut blaster pointing horizontally right, side view. Assets must be fully separate and not touch their cell boundaries. All trees feet near bottom of own cell, complete silhouettes. No ground, no cast shadows, no scenery, no text, no labels, no grid lines, no watermark. Genuine transparent alpha background. Restrained detail, bold readable at 80px tall, game sprite artwork not painted realism.

## UI atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production Unity UI sprite atlas on genuinely transparent background. ONE landscape sheet in exactly 4 equal columns and 2 equal rows, each item centered with at least 40 pixels transparent gutter inside its own cell. Style reference image is guidance for soft hand-drawn mobile cartoon UI only; independently draw original shapes, no tracing. Warm dark chocolate-brown thick smooth outlines, round corners, simple flat pastel fill, minimal soft highlight and small darker bottom edge. Every panel seen perfectly front-on, no perspective. ROW 1: (1) BLANK mint green rounded rectangular upgrade button, width roughly 1.5 times height, dark brown outline, pale mint top highlight, darker mint bottom lip; (2) BLANK warm pale cream rounded rectangular menu panel, subtle sparse tan freckles confined to edge; (3) BLANK honey-brown rounded rectangular equipment card, dark border, slightly inset inner surface; (4) BLANK narrow cream horizontal currency pill, thick brown outline. ROW 2: (1) cute green banknote icon tilted slightly up-right, simple leaf emblem NOT text; (2) bright cyan faceted diamond gem outlined brown; (3) compact orange-red impact/power badge with three small upward points and cream central burst; (4) two small crossed pale-blue blades with golden handles, compact speed icon. No writing, no numbers, no labels, no extra objects, no background, no shadows outside silhouettes, no watermark. Flat clean UI assets readable at mobile size. Fill each cell only with its own asset, with clean transparent separation. Panels must have large uninterrupted blank central fill for live game text.
